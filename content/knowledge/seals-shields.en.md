## Contact seals protect, but add friction

`2RS` variants use contact sealing lips that retain grease and exclude contamination. They generally increase friction and reduce the permissible speed compared with open bearings.

## Shields are non-contact barriers

`2Z` metal shields reduce large-particle ingress without a rubbing seal. They suit applications where some air exchange or lower running torque is required.

## Open bearings need a system-level lubrication plan

Open designs expose the raceway and cage directly. Oil circulation, grease delivery and external sealing become part of the machine design.
