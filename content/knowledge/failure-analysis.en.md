## Preserve the failed components

Do not wash away grease, contamination or wear debris before photographs and material samples are collected. Mark component orientation so the load path can be reconstructed.

## Read the damage morphology

Fatigue spalling, false brinelling, electric erosion, smearing and adhesive wear have different signatures. A single mark should be compared with the full raceway and rolling-element population.

## Trace operating evidence

Review mounting records, alignment, temperature history, vibration trend, lubrication intervals and load changes. A bearing is rarely the root cause merely because it failed.
